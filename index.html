Google Calendar · Read only

Google Tasks · Read & write

Read your task lists, add tasks, mark them complete, and change due dates.

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/tasks

Your short-lived Google token stays in this browser's localStorage. Requests go directly to Google. No backend, database, or shared sessions. Sign out clears Google data from this app. Anyone with access to this browser profile may access your session.

Your inbox, with permission.

Optional Gmail access, separate from Calendar and Tasks. Google grants read-only access to your mailbox, including message content and settings. This dashboard only requests sender, subject, date and message IDs for up to five matching messages. It cannot send, delete, or mark messages read.

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly

The short-lived token stays in this browser’s localStorage. Email headers stay in memory and are never cached. Requests go directly to Google. The Gmail account may differ from your Calendar account; its address is shown on the card. Anyone using this browser profile may access the session.
